Backend Engineer (Node.js)

Brussels, hybrid (two office days a week). Full-time, permanent.

About the role

Woodgrove Analytics builds forecasting tools for regional energy suppliers. You will join a team of six engineers that owns the ingestion pipeline and the public API, and you will help us move our nightly batch jobs to an event-driven design.

What you will do

  • Design and build REST and event-driven services in TypeScript on Node.js.
  • Model data in PostgreSQL and keep slow queries under control.
  • Write tests first, review your colleagues' code and share on-call duty one week in six.
  • Work with product and data science to turn forecasts into features customers use every day.

What you bring

  • Three or more years of backend development with Node.js or a similar runtime.
  • Solid SQL and experience running PostgreSQL in production.
  • Comfort with Docker, CI pipelines and cloud hosting.
  • Good written English; Dutch or French is a plus.

What we offer

A competitive salary, meal vouchers, a hospitalisation insurance plan, 32 days of leave and a yearly training budget of EUR 2,000.
