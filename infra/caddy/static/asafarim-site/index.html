ASafarIM OS

A small core platform for business applications, and apps that plug into it. The core handles what every app needs: routing, sign-in, permissions, configuration, events and monitoring. Each app describes itself, owns its own data and talks to the others through events.

Where it stands

  • Being built in the open, one shippable step at a time.
  • The current ASafarIM apps keep running on asafarim.com while they move over.
  • Nothing to sign up for here yet.
